OPERATOR GUIDE

01 · DETECT & TRACK

Wait for two sweeps. Gray detections hold their last measured position. AUTO TRACK creates up to 24 tracks; dotted circles mark tracks. Type and allegiance need separate evidence.

02 · SELECT & FIRE

Tap a radar contact, a TWS row, or NEXT TARGET. Select Launcher 1–4. MIM-225A fires from a usable track within 305 km. A hard lock is optional for this active missile.

03 · WATCH GUIDANCE

Dotted battery lines mean supported midcourse flight. SEARCHING means the onboard seeker is active but has no target yet. A missile-to-target line appears after SEEKER LOCK.

04 · CHANNELS & SENSOR LOSS

Eight missiles can receive external support. A ninth launch transfers the oldest channel. The displaced active missile searches early. RADAR OFF, RESET, and expired tracks can also trigger early search.

05 · SURVIVE THE EXERCISE

Intercept aircraft before they reach the battery. The battery survives four attack hits. Empty launchers reload from reserve. Tap the selected launcher to reload early. Use 1× / 2× / 4× / 8× time.
